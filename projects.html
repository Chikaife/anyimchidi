A productivity app for tracking tasks, deadlines, and priorities without making planning feel heavy. The interface keeps the next action visible and the overall workload easy to scan.

Its value is in the everyday details: clear hierarchy, quick recognition, and a workflow that supports progress instead of adding friction.

03 / Everyday finance

beeapp

beeapp explores a single, seamless home for airtime, data, bill payments, gift cards, and flight bookings. It treats a busy fintech dashboard as a place for confidence, not confusion.

The project gave me room to think about grouping, trust, visual rhythm, and how many useful actions can share one experience without competing for attention.

  • Information architecture
