The person behind the interface

Curious by nature. Practical by craft.

I am Anyim Chidi, an aspiring full-stack developer from Lagos, Nigeria. I like turning rough ideas into digital experiences that feel clear, useful, and alive.

A little context

"The best way to learn technology is to use it to solve something real."

I learn by building.

My path into software development has been shaped by self-learning, hands-on projects, and a steady curiosity about how products work behind the screen. Each project gives me another chance to make something more responsive, more accessible, and more thoughtful.

I enjoy the meeting point between frontend craft and backend logic: the moment when a visual idea becomes a working system. I am especially interested in web apps, automation, learning systems, and the small details that make an experience easier to use.

What I bring

Make it clear I turn complex requirements into interfaces people can understand quickly.
Make it work I care about reliable interactions, useful feedback, and the logic underneath.
Keep growing I stay open to better tools, new patterns, and lessons from every build.

Beyond the code

Technology is also a way to contribute.

As Director of Software for the Nigeria Association of Computing Students, I coordinated technical projects, supported student developers, and helped manage digital platforms. That experience taught me that good software is collaborative long before it reaches a browser.

When I am away from the keyboard, I am usually exploring a new technology, improving an old project, or looking for a sharper question to ask.
